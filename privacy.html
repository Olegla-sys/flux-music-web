Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Information processed

Flux Music may process identifiers needed for Discord and TikTok integrations, basic profile information made available through authorized scopes, track metadata, service settings, and authorization tokens required to provide connected features.

How information is used

Information is used to operate Flux Music features, identify authorized users, maintain creator workflows, and transfer content requested by the user to supported services.

TikTok integration

When you authorize TikTok, Flux Music only requests permissions needed for enabled features. Content is uploaded only as part of an authorized workflow initiated through the service.

Storage and security

Integration credentials and authorization data are intended to be stored privately and are not displayed publicly. Reasonable measures are used to limit unauthorized access.

Sharing

Flux Music does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to perform a feature you request.

Retention and access

Operational data may be retained while needed to provide the service. Users may revoke connected-platform authorization using the controls provided by that platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Flux Music develops or its integrations change.
